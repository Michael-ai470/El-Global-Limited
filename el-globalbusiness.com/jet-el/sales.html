Overview

Acquire with Precision

Purchasing an aircraft is one of the most significant financial decisions in aviation. The market is opaque, pricing varies dramatically by region and timing, and the technical due diligence required to protect your investment demands specialized expertise. Jet-El provides independent advisory that places your interests at the center of every transaction.

Our team combines deep market intelligence with financial structuring expertise to guide you through the full acquisition lifecycle. We begin with a detailed needs assessment, matching your operational requirements and budget to the right aircraft type. From there, we conduct a global market search, identify acquisition targets, and manage the entire due diligence process including pre-purchase inspections, records review, and regulatory compliance.

We do not sell aircraft. We advise buyers. This distinction eliminates the conflicts of interest that plague traditional brokerage models, ensuring our recommendations are driven by data, market conditions, and your long-term objectives rather than transaction commissions.

How It Works

The Acquisition Process

A disciplined four-phase methodology that protects your capital and delivers the right aircraft for your portfolio.

Needs Assessment

We define your mission profile, operational requirements, budget parameters, and ownership structure preferences to establish clear acquisition criteria before entering the market.

Market Search

Our team conducts a comprehensive search across global listings, off-market opportunities, and our private network to identify aircraft that match your criteria and offer the best value.

Due Diligence

Shortlisted aircraft undergo rigorous evaluation: records audit, pre-purchase inspection, regulatory review, and financial analysis. We present a detailed report with clear recommendations.

Acquisition Closing

We manage the closing process including final negotiations, escrow coordination, title transfer, registration, and delivery logistics. You take possession of a thoroughly vetted asset.
