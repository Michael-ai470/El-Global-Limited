The Service

Leisure Resorts as Portfolio Assets

Golf resorts sit at the intersection of luxury real estate, hospitality, and leisure. The best properties command premium membership fees, generate consistent accommodation and event revenue, and hold significant land value that appreciates over time. They also carry unique complexities in terms of course maintenance, brand management, and seasonal operations.

The 1% advises investors on acquiring golf and leisure resort assets across established and emerging markets. From championship-caliber courses with international brand affiliations to boutique resort developments with untapped potential, we provide the market intelligence, financial analysis, and operational expertise needed to evaluate these assets with precision.

We do not manage golf resorts. We advise on how to identify the right acquisition targets, assess their true investment potential, structure ownership efficiently, and position them for sustained revenue growth and capital appreciation.

Location Assessment

Comprehensive analysis of resort location against tourism demand drivers, climate suitability, transport connectivity, competitive landscape, and long-term regional development plans that impact asset value.

Brand Valuation

Assessment of existing brand equity, course designer reputation, tournament history, awards, and media profile. We quantify the intangible value that brand recognition adds to resort assets and their earning potential.

Development Potential

Evaluation of expansion opportunities including residential villa plots, additional course development, spa and wellness facilities, and mixed-use components that can significantly enhance total asset value.

How It Works

Our Process

A structured approach from market research through to operational advisory and performance optimization.

Market Research

We analyze global golf and leisure resort markets to identify regions with strong demand fundamentals, favorable investment climates, and assets that present compelling value relative to comparable properties.

Site Evaluation

Selected resorts undergo comprehensive evaluation covering course condition, facility quality, brand positioning, financial performance, staffing, maintenance requirements, and competitive market analysis.

Investment Structuring

We advise on optimal ownership structures, financing arrangements, management agreements, and tax-efficient acquisition frameworks tailored to the specific resort asset and its jurisdiction.

Operational Advisory

Post-acquisition guidance on management team selection, membership strategy, course improvement planning, brand development, and revenue optimization to position the resort for sustained long-term growth.
