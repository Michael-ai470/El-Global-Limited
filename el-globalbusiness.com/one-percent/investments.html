Beyond Conventional Markets

The world's most valuable assets rarely appear on any exchange. Blue-chip art by recognized masters, rare timepieces, vintage automobiles, fine wine collections, and bespoke investment structures represent an asset class that has consistently outperformed traditional markets over the long term while offering portfolio diversification that is uncorrelated with equity or fixed-income cycles.

The 1% provides advisory on identifying, authenticating, valuing, and acquiring these ultra-premium assets. Our network spans private galleries, auction houses, specialist dealers, and family offices, providing access to opportunities that are invisible to conventional investors and require deep sector knowledge to evaluate.

We advise on acquisition strategy, authentication and provenance verification, investment structuring for tax efficiency, and secure storage and insurance arrangements. Every opportunity is evaluated as a portfolio asset, not a standalone purchase.

Our Process

A structured approach from understanding your interests through to acquisition and secure storage.

We begin with a confidential conversation to understand your interests, collecting preferences, investment capacity, and objectives. This profile guides every recommendation we make.
