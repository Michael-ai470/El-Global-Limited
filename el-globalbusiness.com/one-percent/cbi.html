What is CBI

Global Citizenship, Strategically Acquired

Citizenship by Investment programs allow qualified individuals to obtain residency or citizenship in a foreign country through a qualifying investment. These programs are government-approved, legally structured, and designed to attract global capital while offering investors enhanced mobility, tax planning, and security.

El-Global's CBI advisory goes beyond application processing. We evaluate each program against your specific objectives, whether that's visa-free travel access, tax optimization, wealth protection, or legacy planning for future generations. Every recommendation is tailored, compliant, and strategic.
