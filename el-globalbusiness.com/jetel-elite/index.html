Benefits of Leasing

  • No large capital outlay required upfront
  • Predictable monthly costs for budgeting
  • Flexibility to upgrade aircraft at term end
  • No residual value or depreciation risk
  • Potential tax advantages through operating lease structures

Benefits of Owning

  • Asset appreciation potential in select markets
  • Revenue generation through charter-back programs
  • Complete control over maintenance and operations
  • Accelerated depreciation tax benefits
  • No term limitations on aircraft usage

The Advantage

Why JetEl-Elite

Four pillars that define the JetEl-Elite ownership and lease experience.

Asset Appreciation

Select aircraft categories have demonstrated consistent value retention and appreciation, particularly in the heavy and ultra long range segments. We identify assets with the strongest residual value trajectories.

Revenue Generation

When your aircraft is not in use, charter-back programs generate revenue that offsets operating costs. Our managed charter placement targets premium market segments for maximum yield.

Tax Advantages

Aircraft ownership offers significant tax planning opportunities including accelerated depreciation, bonus depreciation provisions, and operating cost deductions. We structure every arrangement to optimise fiscal efficiency.

Lifestyle Access

Beyond the investment thesis, ownership unlocks unmatched freedom: depart on your schedule, from private terminals, with a cabin configured to your exact preferences. Time is the ultimate luxury.
