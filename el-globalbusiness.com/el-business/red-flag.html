What each check looks at

Every check runs only on figures you enter. A blank is left out, never treated as zero.

Profitability and revenue

EBITDA margin (high under 5%, medium under 10%), growth (a fall, or above 40%), the largest customer's share (medium above 20%, high above 40%) and revenue per employee.

Cash and debt

Days for customers to pay (medium above 60, high above 90), days to pay suppliers, and debt against EBITDA (medium above 2.5×, high above 4×).

The score

Each area checked scores 100, 62 or 25 by its worst flag; the score is their average. A first screen, not diligence: El&Rock runs the full review.
