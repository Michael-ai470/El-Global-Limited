How this estimate works

A quick, indicative range from sector multiples: a first view before a full valuation.

1. Earnings multiple

EBITDA times the sector's typical EV/EBITDA range, adjusted for stage (early stage 0.7×, growth 1.15×, turnaround 0.6×) and for growth.

2. Revenue cross-check

Revenue times the sector's EV/Revenue range. Where the two methods overlap, the estimate is sturdier; where they diverge, margins deserve a closer look.

3. Confidence

Rises with how complete the inputs are and how closely the two methods agree. A formal valuation adds DCF, precedent transactions and diligence: El&Rock runs those.
