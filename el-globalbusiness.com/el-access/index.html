The El-Access Advantage

What Is an Empty Leg?

When a private jet flies one passenger to their destination, it often needs to reposition — returning home or traveling to its next booking. These repositioning flights are known as empty legs.

Rather than fly empty and lose revenue, operators list these flights on El-Access at dramatically reduced rates. You get the identical aircraft, the identical service — for a fraction of the cost.
